How it works
From eyes to fleet — in 2 seconds
Three layers working together. No cloud round-trip. No specialist hardware required.
01

Camera watches the eyes

Phone camera or Pi-mounted ESP32-CAM continuously captures the rider's face at 8–15 FPS. MediaPipe tracks 468 facial landmarks every frame.

02

EAR + PERCLOS inference

Eye Aspect Ratio computed from 12 landmarks. Two clinical triggers: sustained closure > 0.8 s (microsleep) or PERCLOS > 30% over 60 s (cumulative fatigue).

03

Alert fires on the helmet

Audio burst (4 beeps, max volume), vibration, and a pulsing red overlay widget visible over Maps or any delivery app — all within 2 seconds.

04

Fleet dashboard updates live

Firebase Firestore pushes rider state in real time. Fleet managers see eye state, PERCLOS %, live camera snapshot, and GPS — no refresh needed.

05

SMS to fleet + emergency contact

On every alert, SMS is sent to both the fleet manager and the rider's emergency contact. Works via Android SmsManager or Pi SIM800L hardware.

06

Immutable alert log

Every alert is written to a Firestore subcollection with timestamp, type, PERCLOS value, and GPS location — for insurance, compliance, and accountability.

SmartHelm App
For the rider on the road
Any Android 12+ phone clipped to the helmet becomes a full safety co-pilot — same detection pipeline as the dedicated Pi hardware.
  • 👁

    Background detection

    Runs as a foreground service — stays active with screen off, over Maps, over Zomato, over any app.

  • 🔴

    Floating alert overlay

    22dp green dot in idle mode. Expands to a full red pulsing pill — "DROWSY — PULL OVER" — on alert.

  • 📱

    Phone number login

    Firebase OTP — rider signs in with their phone number. No passwords, no email, no friction.

  • 🔔

    Personalised calibration

    100-frame open-eye baseline sets custom EAR thresholds per rider — no false alerts for narrow-eyed riders.
